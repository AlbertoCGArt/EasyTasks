## Step 1 — the post

Attach `easytasks_x_clip.mp4`. Attached media does **not** count against the
character limit, so the 276 characters below are all you need to fit.

```
Two years of adding one small thing at a time, every time something in Blender should have been one click and took six.

Easy Tasks: select your pipes, click Pipes. They land in the right collection, inside the hierarchy you already built.

Free, open source, Blender 3.0-5.x.
```

**276 / 280 characters.** Four to spare — don't add anything without cutting
something first.

## Step 2 — reply to your own post

Do this immediately after the post goes live, from the same account.

```
Download, full feature list and shortcuts: https://3dartstuff.com/easytasks/
```

**66 / 280** as X counts it (every URL is billed at 23 characters regardless of
its real length).

Keeping the URL out of the main post is the single highest-leverage thing here.
X suppresses reach on posts that send people off-platform; a reply is not
penalised, and anyone who wants the link will read one reply down.

## Optional — hashtags

Two or three at the very end of the main post, or none at all. X hashtags do

`#b3d` is the one the Blender community actually follows. Note this pushes you
over 280 — you'd need to cut the `Blender 3.0-5.x.` fragment to fit them.

## Two things to check before you hit post

1. **Watch it back on your phone once.** It autoplays muted in-feed, so
   whatever the clip communicates has to land with no sound and at phone size.
   Full Blender UI at 1920 px wide gets small on a 6-inch screen — if the
   collection assignment doesn't read, say so and I'll cut a zoomed version.
2. **Have the reply text on your clipboard first.** The gap between post and
   reply is when the first impressions land, and you want the link there
   already.
