## 6. Thumbnail

Two versions, both 16:9. Upload the **1280×720 JPG** — YouTube caps thumbnails at 2 MB
and 1280×720 is its recommended size; the 2560×1440 PNG is the master to re-crop from
if you ever need it.

| File | Overlay text | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `yt_thumbnail_oneclick_1280x720.jpg` | **ONE CLICK** / *per collection* | **Recommended.** |
| `yt_thumbnail_organize_1280x720.jpg` | **ORGANIZE YOUR SCENE** / *in one click* | If you'd rather be literal. |

**Why "ONE CLICK" over "ORGANIZE YOUR SCENE".** Your title already says *Blender Scene
Organization Add-on*. A thumbnail that also says "organize your scene" spends its one
shot repeating the line directly underneath it. "ONE CLICK" is nine characters at the
largest type either version can carry, it survives the 210 px sidebar better than
anything longer, and paired with the title it says something the title doesn't — that
this is fast, not just that it exists.

Both use the same visual: a scattered pile of grey bars resolving through an orange
arrow into the tidy, colour-tagged tree. That's the whole video in one glance, and it
reads at any size — which is more than most text can claim.

If you're able to A/B test thumbnails, run these two against each other. It's a clean
test of one variable: literal description vs. benefit.
