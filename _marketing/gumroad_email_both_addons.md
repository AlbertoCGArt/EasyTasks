## Body

Two free Blender add-ons, out now.

They came out of the same place — the work that surrounds the modelling rather
than the modelling itself. One keeps a scene organised while you build it. The
other records how you built it. Both are things I use daily on my own
environment and prop work, and both are free and open source.

### While you build — Easy Tasks

Blender's outliner will hold any structure you want. It just won't build it for
you, and it won't keep it once you're three hours into blocking out a level.

- Select your floor meshes, click **Floor** — straight into the right
  collection. Point it at your project root and it uses the hierarchy you
  already built, rather than inventing one beside it.
- **Auto-route** puts new objects where they belong the moment you create them.
- **Face Stretch** colour-codes UV distortion as vertex colours in the viewport
  — blue compressed, green even, red stretched.
- **Stacked UV Detector** finds duplicated UV layouts before they reach a bake.
- **Batch Export** sends every selected object out to its own FBX / OBJ / GLB.

35 tools, on three keys. Blender 3.0 – 5.x.

Get it: https://albertocgart.gumroad.com/l/EasyTasks
Video and details: https://3dartstuff.com/easytasks

### How you built it — SceneCast

Once the scene is tidy, the other half of the problem is showing the work.
SceneCast captures every settled edit as a step and plays the session back as a
scrubbable timeline — a tutorial or a progress timelapse without a screen
recorder and without a video editor.

- Every edit becomes a scrubbable step: geometry, selection, 3D cursor, and the
  keys you pressed.
- Playback glides between your recorded viewpoints instead of hard-cutting.
- Edit-mode steps replay with the edit cage and your original selection.
- Export straight to MP4 or a PNG sequence, with per-step timing.

Blender 4.2+.

Get it: https://albertocgart.gumroad.com/l/SceneCast
Demo clips: https://3dartstuff.com/scenecast

Free and open source under GPL-3.0-or-later — no licence to buy, nothing held

If you only want one: Easy Tasks if you spend your time fighting the outliner,
